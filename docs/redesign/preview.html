One shell, three chromes

Reading this as: a daily-driver media client for phone, tablet, desktop and TV, where the chrome should be calm and the artwork should be loud. Keeping the existing token layer and the shared primitives that already work, replacing the four competing navigation models with one adaptive shell.

DESIGN_VARIANCE 4 MOTION_INTENSITY 4 VISUAL_DENSITY 5 accent Signal Teal theme dark + light

Scope note, stated up front: the design-taste skill that prompted this review is built for landing pages and portfolios, and it puts dense product UI and native mobile explicitly out of scope. So its rules are applied here where they apply (one accent, one radius system, no em-dashes, no AI tells, real images, button and form contrast) and the app's own screens are designed against the platform conventions Flutter already gives us: an adaptive shell, real focus traversal, 44 to 48 px targets.

Why it feels inconsistent, structurally

The dock is not a navigation model, it is a per-page ornament. Each page builds its own Scaffold and stacks its own copy.

Switching sections destroys the section

_navigateToPage pushes only from Home and uses pushReplacement everywhere else. Home to Manga to Books leaves a stack of two. Going back skips a section, and every switch rebuilds the page, so scroll position, catalogue filters and search text are gone.

lib/widgets/common/app_liquid_dock.dart:41-58

There is no shell to fix

A page cannot forget to render the shell the way it can forget to render a dock. That is the whole argument: the fix is not a better dock, it is moving navigation out of the pages and into one frame that owns it.

The destination model

Thirteen peers is the root cause. Content types are not peers of Settings, and they will keep growing. Split into three tiers, one owner per destination, five shell slots at most.

Tier 1. Shell slotspersistent, 4 or 5
Home Browse Library Settings Search

Search takes a slot on phones only. On tablet, desktop and TV it becomes a field pinned at the head of the rail plus a keyboard shortcut, and the rail keeps four rows.

Tier 2. The vertical switcherinside Browse
Movies & TV Live TV Anime Manga Books Audiobooks Music

Seven destinations become one segmented control. Adding an eighth vertical later costs a chip, not a nav slot. Each vertical keeps its own scroll and filter state because Browse switches an index instead of replacing a route.

Tier 3. Entity routespushed, with their own back
Details Player Readers Stream sheet Portal browser

These never get shell chrome, which is the correct answer to "Details has no dock". They are a level below the shell, not a peer of it.

The now-playing bar, which is the real fix for Music and Audiobooks

Music currently ships four private mini-player presets and Audiobooks ships its own transport, which is exactly why neither can host the dock. Move playback to one shell-owned bar. Audio gets full transport: artwork, title, prev and play and next, a 2 px accent progress line, and tap-to-expand. Video gets a resume entry, so closing the player leaves something to come back to instead of nothing.

One component, present in every chrome, above the bottom bar on phone and at the foot of the content column elsewhere. That is the element that makes the app feel seamless rather than stitched: playback state stops belonging to a page.

Why it will stop looking like the app it forked

The largest single cause is that the theme picker changes the app's backgrounds. Preset two is upstream's Amethyst Violet with upstream's near-blacks, so switching to it makes ZPlay look like PlayTorrio by design. Make the picker move only the accent.

Change the accent above: the surfaces, borders and type stay put. That is the difference between a palette picker and a brand. The accent is used as a fill and an indicator only, never as body text, which is what lets one accent work on both the dark and the light ramp.

One accent, six jobs

Signal Teal means one thing everywhere: this is live, active or now. The current nav slot, the focus ring, playback progress, a live channel badge, the selected segment. Nothing else gets teal, so the colour teaches the app.

Retire upstream's theatre

The 380 ms circular liquid reveal on every push, the jelly magnification that needs a pointer, and a splash overlay that replays on every launch are upstream's signature moves. Replace with a fade-through and one skippable cold-start brand moment.

Light mode becomes possible

The theme hardcodes Brightness.dark and no file mentions a light brightness, which blocks light mode and blocks SegmentedTabs on a light surface. Every colour is already a semantic token, so the ramp is the only thing missing.

One radius rule

Cards 14, controls and inputs 10, pills fully round, sheets 20 on the top corners only. Fourteen ad-hoc radii collapse to one documented rule, which is what makes screens read as one product.

Density stays where it helps

Chrome calms down, content stays dense: rails keep nine to twelve cards visible, server rows keep their badges, and tabular figures stop timers and sizes from jittering as they animate.

Delete the dead

PlayfairDisplay-SemiBoldItalic ships in the bundle and is referenced nowhere in lib/. Adding a decorative italic serif next to a geometric sans is also the exact mixed-family move the taste rules ban. Drop it, or give it one deliberate job.

Order of work

Each phase ships on its own and is visible on its own. The enabling work comes first because every later phase gets cheaper as a result.

1

Shell skeleton, behind the current entry point

  • Form-factor classifier, TV detector, and the reduced-motion branch the motion scale is missing
  • Shell widget: rail plus content plus now-playing, keeping the four slots alive in an IndexedStack so section state survives
  • Route entity screens above the shell, leaving the current pages untouched inside it
Acceptance: switch slots four times and return. Scroll position, filters and search text are all still there, and Back is the same gesture from every slot.
2

Now-playing bar, and Music and Audiobooks hand over their transport

  • One shell-owned playback bar reading the existing music and audiobook services
  • Delete Music's four mini-player presets and its 240 px sidebar, delete the Audiobooks transport
  • Video gets a resume entry so leaving the player is not a dead end
Acceptance: start audio in Music, navigate to Settings, and the transport is still there and still playing.
3

Browse and the vertical switcher, then delete the duplicates

  • Seven verticals behind one segmented control built from the existing SegmentedTabs
  • Delete Home's Anime tab and its second anime content model
  • Fold Catalog, Calendar, MultiNutz and anime-arabic in as views of a vertical
Acceptance: every one of the 13 old dock destinations is reachable in at most two actions from the shell, and exactly once.
4

Token migration, screen by screen, on the shells that now exist

  • Delete the three private _Space and _Palette copies and the 423 literals, starting with Details then Settings then the verticals
  • Fill in the missing Material sub-themes so components inherit instead of being restyled per screen
  • Extract the shared empty, loading, error and section-header components the findings already call for
Acceptance: changing the accent repaints the whole app correctly. Zero literal hex colours outside provider brand marks.
5

Identity pass, last because it is the cheapest

  • Accent-only theming, and a real light ramp
  • Retire the liquid reveal, the splash replay and the jelly-only magnification
  • Drop the unused display serif, or give it exactly one role
Acceptance: no preset makes the app look like its upstream, and light mode passes the same contrast checks as dark.
